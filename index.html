The office is on fire. Bring bombs.

Workday Blast is a 3D Bomberman-style game for Android where the coworkers are the heroes and Monday is the final boss. Look around, try the mini demo, then help us test the real thing.

Main menu: Play Offline, Story Mode or Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer.

Six chapters of Operation Chaos

A saboteur has turned the company’s own systems against the staff. Pick a chapter: the whole page takes on that map’s colours, just like the game’s themes.

Not just a story

Whether you love blowing things up or just like cute worker characters, there is a way in.

Story mode

Briefings, radio chatter, stars per chapter, bosses and a bonus Office Maze with switch plates and gates.

Battle mode

You against 1 to 3 bots on big 33 × 29 maps with hills, ladders, water and a fog-of-war minimap.

Wi-Fi multiplayer

Same network, no sign-in: play against friends in the same room.

20 coworkers

Coder, Accountant, Construction Worker and more, each with a special ability and their own look.

Try the idea

Mini demo. This is a simplified 2D version that only recreates the concept of the game: drop bombs, break crates, dodge flames. The real game is 3D and has far more in it (story, bosses, abilities, hills and hazards).

Become a tester

 

 
What do I need?
An Android phone with Google Play and a Google account signed in. It takes about a minute.

 
Is it free? Do I need an account?
No account is needed to play. Your progress and settings are saved on your phone.

 
How do I send feedback?
Email what you were doing and what happened. Screenshots help, and confusing moments are useful feedback too.
