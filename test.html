Become a tester in 3 steps

It takes about a minute. Use the Google account you are signed in with on your Android phone.

Join the tester group

This tells Google Play you are allowed to test. Sign in with the same Google account as your phone, then press "Join group".

Join the Google Group

Opt in on Google Play

Open this link on your Android phone and press "Become a tester". It only works after step 1.

Open the test page

Install and play

Install Workday Blast from Google Play, play a few rounds, and keep it installed for the whole test period (14 days). Google counts installed days.

Open on Google Play

If something does not work

  • The Play page says "not available": wait a few minutes after joining the group, and check you use the same Google account on the phone.
  • You opened the link on a computer: open it on your Android phone instead.
  • Still stuck: email the developer with a screenshot.

What to tell me

Anything that crashes, feels slow, is confusing, or is too hard or too easy. Mention your phone model and which chapter or mode you were in.
