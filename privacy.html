Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026. This is a plain-language policy for the Workday Blast Android game ("the game").

The short version

The game has no accounts and no developer server. Your progress and settings stay on your phone.

What is stored on your device

  • Story progress: unlocked chapters, stars and times.
  • Settings: chosen character, map, sound and similar options.

This stays on your device. The developer cannot see it.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and it does not send gameplay data to the developer.

Wi-Fi multiplayer

Multiplayer connects phones on the same local network. Game data (positions, bombs, choices) is exchanged only between the players in that match.

Ads and third parties

The game currently shows no ads and uses no analytics. If an optional rewarded ad is added in the future, this policy will be updated first to name the ad provider and what it collects.

Google Play

If you install through Google Play, Google handles the install and may collect data under its own policy. Joining the tester group shares your Google account email with the group manager so you can be added to the test.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Workday Blast > Storage. Everything saved by the game is removed.

Contact

Questions: email the developer.
